ZipLeague: Simple and extendible ranking platform

2025

ZipLeague is a simple and extendible ranking platform built with Django and Docker, built to track SpikeBall matches held during launch break in our lab. It allows users to create and manage matches, track scores, and display rankings in real-time. The platform is designed to be easily customizable and scalable, making it suitable for various types of competitions.

Django CI/CD Opensource
