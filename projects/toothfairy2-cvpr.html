Segmenting Maxillofacial Structures in CBCT Volumes

Conference CVPR 2025

ToothFairy2 dataset description released together with strong benchmark results for the segmentation of 42 maxillofacial structures in Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) volumes. The dataset has been used in the homonymous MICCAI 2024 challenge and at the time was the largest public dataset for maxillofacial segmentation.
