Segmenting the Inferior Alveolar Canal in CBCTs Volumes: the ToothFairy Challenge

Journal TMI 2024

Organizing and leading a segmentation challenge for MICCAI, based on cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) volumes, fostering innovation in dental AI and providing the first public benchmark datasets for the community. The challenge has been part of MICCAI 2023 and the results were published in IEEE Transations on Medical Imaging Journal.
